# Brag Plan: Guess The List

## What is this app?
A suite of **34 daily football guessing games** built around the golden era (~1990–2010), where clues drip in — club badges, squad numbers, positions and flags — until a player's name clicks.

## The angle
Not "a quiz app" — *the* daily football guessing games, matchday-graphics clean. The brag leans on two true, specific things: the **clue-drip mechanic** (badges + flags + a transfer fee resolve into a name) and the **sheer breadth** (34 games, from Two Clubs to Record Signings to the Ballon d'Or). It should feel like a Sky-Sports/FPL promo for a product made by someone who clearly loves football's golden era, with a pub-nostalgia warmth under the gloss.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black. A football pitch-grid glimmers in. The wordmark **GUESS THE LIST** slams in — "LIST" lit green with a glow — on the first strong beat. Kicker underneath: *"The daily football guessing games."* The green glow + the Russo One wordmark is the identity; leading with it is the hook.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The clue drips in:** a glass guess-panel where a position chip `DF`, an Argentina flag, and a Roma crest arrive one by one, then a transfer line `Roma → Real Madrid · €51.2m` — then the answer row flips **green with a check** and the name lands. The product *doing its thing*.
- **The breadth montage:** `34 GAMES · ONE GOLDEN ERA` over a beat-synced cascade of real game tiles (glass cards, their actual icons + names): Guess the XI, Two Clubs, Record Signings, Nationality Players, Only Player, Football Families, Ballon d'Or, World Cup, Who Scored More…
- **The pub line:** "Brilliant solo. Better down the pub." — the product's actual personality.

## Outro / punchline
Trophy glow: **"Complete every game. Win a shirt."** (a real promise from the site), then the wordmark settles with its green glow. Clean logo hit on a strong beat.

## User flow worth showing
entry → key action → result, recreated faithfully:
1. A guess panel for one round (nationality × club).
2. Clues drip in one by one: position, flag, crest, transfer fee.
3. The name is entered and the row resolves **correct** (green, check, player name).
This clue→name resolution is the heart of every game and the centerpiece of the video.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: slick matchday-graphics promo — Sky Sports / FPL energy with pub-nostalgia warmth
- Interpretation: clean glass feature-cards, confident green-on-dark, smooth slides and wipes, zero clutter; energy comes from snappy entrances and a beat-synced tile cascade, never from yanking text away before it can be read.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: ~19s

- Accent: `#22c55e` (green-500), highlight `#4ade80`; green glow `text-shadow 0 0 28px rgba(34,197,94,.45)`
- Text: `#ffffff`, sub-text white @ ~70%; button text on green `#0b1020`
- Display font: Russo One
- Body/UI font: Chakra Petch (UI), Noto Sans (body)
- Strongest visual element: dark pitch-grid + green glow blooms + frosted glass cards + floating clue chips (`DF + 🇦🇷 + Roma crest → Real Madrid €51.2m`)

## Share copy (draft)
34 daily football guessing games for the golden era — badges, flags and squad numbers drip in until the name clicks. Brilliant solo, better down the pub. ⚽

## Audio direction
- Role: upbeat rhythmic bed, clean and confident (matchday-promo)
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM)
- Music treatment: start at 0, quick fade-in, steady confident level, duck ~2–3 dB under the "correct" chime, fade out over the last ~0.8s
- Music cue guidance: strong cues at **1.60s** (wordmark slam), **3.70s / 5.80s** (clue chips), **6.34s / 8.96s** (tile cascade); target a strong beat for the final logo hit. Beat grid ~0.52s apart — snap sequential TEXT to every other beat (~1.05s) and hold the full set after.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — bass/RMS makes the green glow and panel presence breathe; no waveform bars.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched, premium restraint — soft UI tick per clue chip, one satisfying "correct" chime on the green reveal, light whoosh per tile in the cascade, one clean logo hit at the end.
- Audio-coupled moments: clue chips (sequential ticks), the correct reveal (chime), tile cascade (whooshes on the beat grid), final wordmark (logo hit).
- Restraint rule: no stadium-crowd cliché, no SFX on every frame; the "correct" chime is the one emotional peak and must stay clean.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Wordmark hook — 2.5s
Dark pitch-grid background with a soft green glow bloom top-left. **GUESS THE LIST** slams in centre (Russo One), "LIST" green with glow. Kicker fades in beneath: *"The daily football guessing games."* Hold the line long enough to read.
Sequential/interaction: none (single slam)
Audio intent: a short riser resolving on the first strong beat — arrival, confidence
Audio-coupled idea: logo-hit style accent on the wordmark slam (~1.60s cue)
Music: upbeat bed fades in
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The clue drips in (product in use) — 5s
A frosted glass "guess" panel titled with a round, e.g. *Argentina × AS Roma*. Clues arrive one by one: `DF` position chip → Argentina flag → Roma crest → a transfer line `Roma → Real Madrid · €51.2m`. A guess field sits below; the answer row then flips **green with a check** and the player name settles in. Caption fragment: *"…until the name clicks."*
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 clue chips arrive one by one snapped to the beat grid, each held ~0.8s; then the correct-row reveal
Audio intent: anticipation building to a satisfying resolution
Audio-coupled idea: soft UI tick per chip; a clean "correct" chime on the green reveal
Music: steady bed, ducks slightly under the chime
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Breadth montage — 5.5s
Heading slams: **34 GAMES · ONE GOLDEN ERA**. Beneath, a cascade of real game tiles (glass cards, each with its actual lucide icon + name) slides in on the beat: Guess the XI · Two Clubs · Record Signings · Nationality Players · Only Player · Football Families · Ballon d'Or · World Cup · Who Scored More. Tiles stagger in, then the full grid holds.
Sequential/interaction: yes — tiles cascade snapped to every other beat (~1.05s) so names stay readable; whole grid holds ~1s at the end
Audio intent: driving, momentum, "there's so much here"
Audio-coupled idea: light whoosh per tile arrival, aligned to the beat grid
Music: fullest section of the bed
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The era + the pub — 3s
Two floating clue chips drift (a flag+crest pairing). Big line: **Football's golden era. 1990–2010.** Subline: *"Brilliant solo. Better down the pub."*
Sequential/interaction: none (lines hold)
Audio intent: warm, settling, personable
Audio-coupled idea: none (let the bed carry it)
Music: sustained
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Payoff / outro — 3s
Trophy icon with a green glow. Line: **Complete every game. Win a shirt.** Then the **GUESS THE LIST** wordmark settles centre with its green glow and holds.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: confident button — the promise, then the logo
Audio-coupled idea: one clean logo hit on a strong beat as the wordmark settles
Music: resolve and fade out over the last ~0.8s
Transition mood: clean hold (end)
